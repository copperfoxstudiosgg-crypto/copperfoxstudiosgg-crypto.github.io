Wire Up – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29/10/2026

Wire Up is a circuit puzzle game for Android made by Copper Fox Studios ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: the game works offline and keeps your progress on your phone. It uses Google services for two things: ads (Google AdMob), and leaderboards if you sign in to Google Play Games.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: levels solved, stars, hints, settings (sound, music, vibration), your Daily Circuit results and streak, and leaderboard scores waiting to be sent. This file stays on your device. We don't receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game. Phone backups are turned off for this file.

Google Play Games Services (leaderboards)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game sends your Daily Circuit times and your total star count to Google Play Games leaderboards. Google then processes your Play Games profile (your gamer name, avatar and player ID) and these scores. Other players can see your gamer name and scores on the leaderboards, depending on your Play Games visibility settings. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play the whole game without signing in; scores just won't be sent.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for a free hint, and occasionally a full-screen ad between levels (never during a puzzle). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob collects information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with the ads. Google may use this to show you personalised ads, depending on your choices.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account of our own.
  • We don't run our own servers or collect analytics.
  • We don't sell personal information, and we don't receive the data AdMob collects in a form that identifies you.
  • The game needs internet access only for ads and Google Play Games. Without internet it plays normally, with no ads.

Purchases

The game doesn't offer in-app purchases yet. If we add them, payments will be handled entirely by Google Play, and we will update this policy first.

Children

Wire Up is meant for players aged 13 and over, and its ads are not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Leaderboards use Google Play Games, which follows Google's own rules for younger users.

Changes

If the game starts using information in a new way (for example, adding in-app purchases), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
